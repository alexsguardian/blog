### Blog Disclaimers

Being a Senior DevOps Engineer I get much more exposure to the industry, and as such, I need to make clear that my opinions are my own and not those of my employer, or the company I run (<a href="https://alexsguardian.com">Alex's Guardian</a>).

I have also added a disclaimer about AI use and this blog. Following <a href="https://responsibleaidisclosure.com/">RAID or the Responsible AI Disclosure guidelines</a>. I do not use AI to generate content for this blog, but I do use it to help me with grammar if LanguageTool doesn't catch it. It also helps me maintain the actual code base of this blog as I, unfortunately, have limited time to do overhauls of pages, updating dependencies that break things, as well as keeping the site updated with the latest Tailwind/TypeScript/Astro versions. The theme I built this blog on, Astrowind, appears to be having a bumpy patch when it comes to updates. No idea if v2 will ever land.

Anyway, I wanted to be open and transparent about this usage. I give my word that none of my content will ever be AI generated, as like you, I hate reading it too. Unfortunately, I know this will turn some of you away and that's okay. I won't be mad :slightly_smiling_face:.
